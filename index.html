ABOUT ME

A bit about me

I’m Nish, a Computer Engineering student in Singapore who likes figuring out what software can do with real inputs.

CourtVision started with a basketball question. Could I find the moments worth replaying without scrubbing through a whole clip? I built a tool that tracks players and flags possible contact for someone to review.

At the lab bench, I’ve used a PIC microcontroller to read sensors and control a pump and fan. Outside class, I’ve taught basic coding to kids in my neighbourhood and at my secondary school. I enjoy the moment when something that looked confusing finally makes sense.
