LEADERSHIP & COMMUNITY

Coding mentor 2025 ↗

Introduced children from low-income families in my neighbourhood and at my secondary school to the basics of coding. I broke unfamiliar ideas down into approachable steps and helped make programming feel less intimidating. Sharing what I know is part of why I enjoy technology: a first working program can give someone a reason to keep exploring.

Engineering Studies Club 2025 — NOW ↗

As a committee member, I contributed to the planning and delivery of Freshman Orientation activities, helping new students find their feet in an unfamiliar environment. It’s the people side of engineering: coordinating with a team, communicating clearly, and turning a plan into an experience people actually enjoy.

Engineering Student Ambassadors 2025 — NOW ↗

As a committee member of Engineering Student Ambassadors, I contributed to events that put Temasek Polytechnic in front of prospective students and celebrate its community. From Open House to award ceremonies, I supported event delivery and represented the school through hands-on involvement. It’s an opportunity to take responsibility beyond coursework and be part of how people experience TP.

National Day Parade ↗

As Member-in-Charge at the National Day Parade, I took on an on-site coordination role at one of Singapore’s most significant national events. Working alongside the team to facilitate the event meant staying responsive, communicating clearly, and following through on responsibilities in a live setting. A very different kind of execution from a classroom project — and an experience I’m proud to have contributed to.
